## Recording Setup

**Minimum gear:**
- Phone on a tripod or propped up, pointing down at your desk
- Desk lamp or ring light (controller needs to be well-lit)
- OBS or Xbox Game Bar to screen-record gameplay (60fps)

**Layout for most videos:**
- Top 60% = screen recording (gameplay)
- Bottom 40% = hand-cam showing controller + triggers
- Or: full-screen close-up of controller with PIP game footage

**Audio:**
- Record voiceover separately (phone voice memo app, then sync in CapCut)
- Capture game audio + controller trigger sounds (mic near the desk picks up the click/thump)
