## Description

Turn your PS5 DualSense into a full haptic peripheral for your PC. Adaptive trigger resistance, voice-coil rumble, and lightbar feedback that reacts to the game you're playing — even when the game doesn't natively support it.

**Windows-first. Xbox output. Forza telemetry. F1 23. Assetto Corsa.**

---

### Racing
Real car telemetry from Forza Horizon, Forza Motorsport, F1 23, or Assetto Corsa drives the triggers. Dynamic brake curve simulates hydraulic pedal feel. Throttle resistance firms up with speed (aero downforce). Tire slip feeds wheelspin flutter and lockup judder through the trigger motors. Suspension bumps, kerb strikes, and road surface texture route to the left and right grip motors stereophonically. ABS pump pulses the brake pedal at the lockup threshold. Gear shifts deliver a mechanical clunk through both triggers with rev-matched intensity. Rev-limiter bounce at redline. Trailbraking oversteer feedback. Drivetrain auto-detection picks the right DSP profile for your car.

### Gun
Nine weapon profiles: pistol, revolver, rifle, burst, AR, SMG, LMG, shotgun, sniper. Semi-auto trigger break at 43% pull with a recoil kick. Burst fire with configurable intra-burst rate. Full-auto continuous vibration at adjustable fire rate. Free tier locks to semi-auto — Full unlocks everything.

### Melee
Ten weapon profiles themed on Dead Island 2. Resting swing heft builds resistance as you pull R2. At full draw, release fires a connect-kick impact thump through the trigger and both grip motors. Fists, knife, machete, katana, axe, cleaver, knuckles, bat, spear, sledgehammer.

### Audio Reactive
System audio drives the haptics. Two-band split: sub (<90 Hz, kicks/impacts) routes to the left grip motor, engine band (90-280 Hz, revs/tone) routes to the right. Reactive rumble mode works on any platform. TRUE haptics mode streams the waveform directly into the DualSense's USB audio channels (Windows, channels 3/4) — the same path PS5 games use.

### Static
Fixed trigger resistance. Light up the controller's lightbar. No game required.

### Minecraft
Fabric mod connects via local TCP. Per-item lightbar colors and trigger feels for every tool and weapon. Sword/axe swing impact, pickaxe mining grind, bow draw tension, shield brace, eating gulp, low-health heartbeat, sprint footfalls.
